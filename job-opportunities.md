## How this list was built, and its limits

- The cloud environment that made this list can't reach the user's Chrome browser or LinkedIn login. Direct access to linkedin.com was also blocked by its network policy. Already-applied and closed jobs could not be filtered out automatically. Use `check-jobs.js` (see above) to find them.
- Jobs were found through web search results that index public LinkedIn job pages. The descriptions are summaries based on the job title, the search snippet and the CV. They are not copied from the full job descriptions.
- Posting dates come from search results or are estimated from the LinkedIn job ID.
- Postings from before 2026 were left out as almost certainly closed. That includes Virtusa, LSEG, Allion, ZOOMi and Superloop, plus the 2025 jobs removed above.
