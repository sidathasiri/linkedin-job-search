## How this list was built, and its limits

- The cloud environment that made this list can't reach the user's Chrome browser or LinkedIn login. Direct access to linkedin.com was also blocked by its network policy. **Already-applied jobs have not been filtered out.** Check each link: LinkedIn shows an "Applied" badge on jobs you've applied to.
- Jobs were found through web search results that index public LinkedIn job pages. The descriptions are summaries based on the job title, the search snippet and the CV. They are not copied from the full job descriptions.
- Posting dates are estimated from the LinkedIn job ID. Some jobs may be closed; LinkedIn shows "No longer accepting applications" on those.
- Postings from before 2025 (for example Virtusa, LSEG, Allion, ZOOMi, Superloop) were left out as almost certainly closed.
